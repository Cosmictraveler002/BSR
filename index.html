Our Story

Preserving Heritage Through Every Bite.

বাঙালির শখের রান্নাঘর began with a simple passion: rediscovering forgotten recipes from grandmother's handwritten journals and presenting them with artisanal culinary elegance.

Our minimalist ambient space removes unnecessary clutter, allowing the vibrant aroma of mustard, fresh spices, and ghee to take center stage. Every dish is cooked with love and historical precision.

Welcome to বাঙালির শখের রান্নাঘর. We value your trust and are committed to protecting your personal privacy when dining with us or visiting our digital platforms.

1. Information We Collect

When you book a table, place an online order, or register for private dining events, we collect essential details such as your name, phone number, email address, date, and party size to fulfill your reservation.

2. Use of Information

Your details are used solely to confirm restaurant reservations, communicate order statuses, provide customer support, and enhance your culinary dining experience at our Kalyani location.

3. Data Protection & Confidentiality

We implement strict security measures to ensure your personal data remains safe. We do not sell, trade, or share your private information with third parties under any circumstances.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding our privacy practices, feel free to contact us at admin@bangalirsokherrannaghor.in or call +91 33 2456 7890.
